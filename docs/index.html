A clean home screen for your TV.

TV Home replaces your TV's ad-filled home screen with a simple, beautiful one: your favourite apps in a dock, a big clock, the weather and what's playing. Installs with the remote in a few minutes. No phone or computer needed. Free and open source, in 12 languages.

Install it with the remote

  1. Get DownloaderOn the TV, open the Play Store (on Fire TV: Amazon's app store), search for Downloader by AFTVnews and install it.
  2. Type the codeOpen Downloader. In the box, type the code
    2960939
    and press Go. If Downloader asks to be allowed to install apps, choose Settings, switch it on, and press Back.
    No luck with the code? Type this instead: g41us.github.io/tv-vibehack/get
  3. Install and openChoose Install, then Open.
  4. Do it all for meTV Home shows a short setup screen. Press Do it all for me and follow the three steps on screen:
    • Turn on Developer options: Settings → System (or Device Preferences) → About → press OK 7 times on Build.
    • Turn on USB debugging in Developer options (some TVs call it Network or ADB debugging).
    • Press Set everything up. When the TV asks "Allow USB debugging?", tick Always allow and choose OK.
    That's it. Press Home: TV Home opens, and the TV starts in it from now on.
    Rather not turn on debugging? Use the other buttons on the same screen: Home button and start-up → Turn on (choose TV Home in Accessibility), then Allow and Allow.

What you get

  • No ads or sponsored rows. Just your apps, with your four favourites in a dock.
  • Starts in TV Home, and the Home button always comes back to it.
  • Clock, date and weather for any city in the world, with a forecast for the week.
  • Now playing: what's on Spotify or YouTube Music, with a full-screen player and lyrics.
  • Quick panel: hold Back anywhere for music, volume, night mode, sleep and Speed up.
  • Night mode: a softer screen and a volume limit late at night.
  • Wallpapers: built in, gently moving, or a photo from your phone.
  • Guest Wi-Fi QR code and the data used today and this month.
  • Updates itself: new versions install with one press.
  • In your language: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Catalan, Arabic, Hindi, Swahili, Indonesian, Thai and Chinese.

Which TVs it works on

TVs and boxes that run Android TV or Google TV: Sony, TCL, Hisense (Android models), Xiaomi, Philips (Android models), Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield, the Airtel Smart Router and most Android boxes. If something doesn't work on yours, open TV check in TV Home and send a report: it shows what to fix.

Amazon Fire TV (Fire OS 7 and 8, so most Fire TV Sticks and Fire TV TVs): works too. Expect a short flash of Amazon's home when you press Home, and on Fire OS 7 a wait of a few seconds. For Do it all for me on Fire TV, press OK 7 times on the device name in About, then turn on ADB debugging. Newer Fire TVs on Amazon's Vega OS can't install apps from outside Amazon's store.

It can't work on Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku, Hisense VIDAA or Apple TV. They don't run Android apps, and they don't let any app take over the home screen. The easy fix for those TVs: plug in an Android TV or Google TV stick and use TV Home on that.

Using it

Hold OK on an app to move it in the dock, add it, or remove it. The dock holds four.
Hold OK on Settings (top right) for wallpaper, weather city, music, night mode, updates and setup.
Hold Back, anywhere, for the Quick panel.
Press OK on the weather for the week's forecast.

Questions

Why does it need Accessibility?

Many TVs open their own home screen at start-up and on the Home button, whatever app you choose. TV Home's Accessibility switch only watches for that screen and a few buttons, and opens TV Home instead. It does not read, record or send anything on your screen. Android shows a general warning for every app that asks.

Does it remove or break my TV's apps?

No. Nothing is uninstalled or disabled. Your channels, apps and settings stay exactly as they were.

How do I go back to the old home screen?

Settings → Accessibility → TV Home → off. To remove it completely: Settings → Apps → TV Home → Uninstall. The TV goes straight back to how it was.

A system update brought the old home screen back.

Open TV Home from the app list and do the setup again (hold OK on Settings → Updates & setup).

Something isn't working.

Hold OK on Settings → Updates & setup → TV check. It shows a ✓ or ✗ for each part of TV Home and what to press to fix it. Send a report there shows a QR code: scan it with your phone to send the details of your TV, so support for it can be added.

Is it safe?

Yes. The code is open on GitHub, nothing is flashed or rooted, and updates only install when they're signed with TV Home's own key, so nobody else can push a fake update to your TV.

I reinstalled TV Home. Are my settings gone?

No. TV Home keeps an encrypted backup of your dock, wallpaper, weather and Wi-Fi QR on the TV. After reinstalling, press Allow next to "Keep your settings safe" on the setup screen and they come back.

Extras for people with a phone or computer

With ADB (Termux on Android, or a computer) the airtel-fix.sh script does the same setup from a phone, plus faster animations and SmartTube. Add ADBLOCK=1 for ad-blocking DNS on the whole box (it can stop some boxes reaching the internet). Not needed for TV Home itself.
