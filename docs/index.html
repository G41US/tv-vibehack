For the Airtel Smart Router TV box

A clean home screen for your Airtel box.

TV Home replaces the box's ad-filled home screen with a simple, beautiful one: your favourite apps in a dock, a big clock, the weather and a few wallpapers. Installs with the remote in about three minutes. No phone, computer or developer settings needed. Free and open source.

The TV Home look. Your dock shows the apps you choose.
Download TV Home (APK)

On the TV, the easiest way is the Downloader app. See the steps below.

Install it with the remote

  1. Get DownloaderOn the Airtel box, open the Play Store, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Download TV HomeOpen Downloader. In the address box type
    g41us.github.io/tv-vibehack/get
    and press Go. If Downloader asks to be allowed to install apps, choose Settings, switch it on, and press Back.
  3. Install and openChoose Install, then Open.
  4. Two quick switchesTV Home shows a short setup screen:
    • Home button and start-up → Turn on. In Accessibility, choose TV Home, turn it on, press Back.
    • Updates from the TV → Allow.
    That's it. Press Home: TV Home opens, and the box starts in it from now on.

What you get

  • No ads or sponsored rows. Just your apps, with your four favourites in a dock.
  • Starts in TV Home and the Home button always comes back to it.
  • Clock, date and the weather for your city.
  • Wallpapers: six built in, or send a photo from your phone.
  • Guest Wi-Fi QR code: visitors scan the TV to join your Wi-Fi.
  • Data used today and this month.
  • Updates itself: new versions install with one press.
  • A clock screensaver that drifts so nothing burns in.

Using it

Hold OK on an app to move it in the dock, add it, or remove it. The dock holds four.
Hold OK on Settings (top right) for wallpaper, weather city, updates and setup.
Hold OK on the network button for the guest Wi-Fi QR code and data used.

Questions

Why does it need Accessibility?

The Airtel box opens its own home screen at start-up and on the Home button, whatever app you choose. TV Home's Accessibility switch only watches for that screen and the Home button, and opens TV Home instead. It does not read, record or send anything on your screen. Android shows a general warning for every app that asks.

Does it remove or break Airtel's apps?

No. Nothing is uninstalled or disabled. Your channels, Airtel account app and settings stay exactly as they were.

How do I go back to Airtel's home screen?

Settings → Accessibility → TV Home → off. To remove it completely: Settings → Apps → TV Home → Uninstall. The box goes straight back to how it was.

An Airtel update brought their home screen back.

Open TV Home from the app list and do the setup again (hold OK on Settings → Setup).

Is it safe?

Yes. The code is open on GitHub, nothing is flashed or rooted, and updates only install when they're signed with TV Home's own key, so nobody else can push a fake update to your box.

Extras for people with a phone or computer

With ADB (Termux on Android, or a computer) the airtel-fix.sh script adds ad-blocking DNS for the whole box, faster animations and SmartTube. Not needed for TV Home itself.
